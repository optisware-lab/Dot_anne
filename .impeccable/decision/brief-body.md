## Scope

Home page (single long page with anchors) for DOT ANNE HOME LODGE. Mode: Persuade.

## Audience and job

Search visitors on phones (business travellers, transit/late arrivals, couples, Tema/Kpone visitors). Job: understand where the lodge is, see the real rooms, and call or WhatsApp to book within seconds.

## Proof on hand

Real photos of the lodge (exterior courtyard, garden, bedroom, two bathrooms, lounge). Google rating 4.2 from 47 reviews. Plus Code and coordinates. No prices, no testimonials, one confirmed room type (Deluxe Double, from Booking.com).

## Constraints

Client-pinned palette and serif+sans pairing. Unconfirmed amenities off by default (config/site.ts). No stock presented as the lodge.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the plan sheet for one plot, No. 473 Golf Estate; every section moves the guest one step nearer its gate. Refuses the centred-headline-over-photo hotel hero and rows of equal amenity cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory drafting paper ground; forest green as the land and the big fields (hero title block, location sheet, footer); muted gold only for hairlines, the route line, plot markers and the primary action; charcoal ink for text. Hairline survey rules, corner registration ticks, sheet title blocks with labelled cells (PLOT, ESTATE, PLUS CODE, DESK), condensed caps labels in Archivo, Gloock display serif for headlines. Real photographs framed like plates pinned to the sheet with a plate caption cell.
