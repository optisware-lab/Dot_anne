public Google Maps listing "DOT ANNE HOME LODGE" (P275+W52 BOST ROAD, Tema), at the site owner's request.

**Before launch, the owner must confirm the lodge owns these photos or has the photographer's permission.**
Google Maps photos can be uploaded by the business or by guests; guest uploads belong to the guest.
If any photo cannot be confirmed, replace it with a new photo of the same subject at the same file name.
